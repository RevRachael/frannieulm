Welcome

Hi, I’m Frannie, and I’m glad you’ve made your way here. Perhaps what brought you is something we share—a sense that the sacred is woven into our lives, sometimes quietly familiar, sometimes inviting us to see beyond what we’ve known.

The words we use to describe the sacred are part of that discovery. I invite us to be curious about those words and the meanings they hold. What might we discover when we look at a familiar word with fresh eyes or listen to what it means to someone else?

Words with spiritual or metaphysical meaning can land differently for each of us, shaped by our experiences, traditions, and beliefs. Exploring their contemporary use, alongside scientific meanings where they intersect with metaphysical inquiry, can offer fresh insight while honoring the distinct perspectives each brings.

Together, we can deepen our conversations, open new possibilities, and grow closer through shared understanding.

About the Author

Rev. Dr. Frannie Ulm is an author, teacher, and licensed minister with the Centers for Spiritual Living, and holds a Doctor of Consciousness Studies from Emerson Theological Institute.

Her years in hospice ministry and her love of words led to her first book, Reimagining the Sacred: A Dictionary for the Modern Seeker.
